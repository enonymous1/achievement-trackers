Wreckfest — Achievement Tracker

Banger racing at its finest — five championships, a garage full of wrecks, and one very patient lawn mower.

Order: Career first, in championship order — the rank, vehicle, and wrecking totals build up as you go, and the rank-50 grind is the cleanup after the campaign (expect to finish the story around rank 30–35). Multiplayer wins need human opponents; wins against AI don't count.

Scope: Full game: all 20 achievements / 1,000G — the Windows (PC Game Pass) version. No TrueAchievements walkthrough exists; route notes are paraphrased from the Knoef and Fuzionbyte trophy guides, which cover the same achievements. About 15–20 hours.

Wrecking & stunts 6 achievements ·

Wreck 100 Opponents.

Wreck 100 opponents — only the final hit counts, so an assist on a 90%-damaged car doesn't. A big tough vehicle against AI lawnmowers in a custom deathmatch is the fastest farm.

Get Wrecked 100 Times.

Get wrecked 100 times — it builds up through the campaign and multiplayer; if you're short, grab a lawnmower and get yourself killed.

Wreck 50 Opponents With School Bus.

Wreck 50 opponents with the School Bus — it unlocks from the Oval Challenge; some campaign missions use it, and the rest can be farmed in private matches against bots.

Spend 5 Minutes In Air.

Spend 5 minutes total in the air — Bone Breaker Valley has two good air spots per lap.

Drive 1 Hour With Lawn Mower.

Drive 1 hour with the Lawn Mower — it unlocks from the Eat Dirt! challenge. Custom event, no AI opponents, nudge it forward, then idle for an hour (keep the controller awake).

Reach 210 Km/h (130 Mph) With Rocket.

Reach 210 km/h (130 mph) with the Rocket, the starter car — find a long straight or downhill stretch.

Multiplayer 1 achievement ·

Win 20 Events In Multiplayer.

Win 20 multiplayer events — boostable in a private lobby with a friend, taking turns winning. Wins against AI opponents do not count.
