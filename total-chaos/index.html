Total Chaos — Achievement Tracker

Washed ashore on Fort Oasis — survive the island, spare what you can, and find the way out.

Order: Sections run in chapter order — Arrival through Resolve — then the merciful run (the five chapter variants plus the good ending), the endings, combat, crafting, and the collectibles. The good ending and the full journal can't be done in one playthrough: a journal in Forgotten sits behind the Protector fight, and killing her locks the good ending out. Plan on at least two runs — a merciful run for the good ending, and a journal run (which pairs well with Survivalist) for the rest.

Scope: All 37 achievements / 1,000G — the full game, no DLC. No TrueAchievements walkthrough exists; route notes are paraphrased from the Twinfinite good-ending walkthrough (Serhii Patskan, Nov 2025) and TA achievement solutions. About 40–50 hours — and the Survivalist run is the hard part.

Complete Decay

Complete Forgotten

Complete Storm

Merciful run 6 achievements ·

Complete Forgotten without killing the Protector

Spare the Protector: lure her to the 'They're gone…' wall so her scream cracks it, then slip out through the secret exit with the fuses. Killing her locks this out for the run.

Complete Storm without killing the Forgotten Ones or burning the Connections

Play Storm without killing a single Forgotten One and without burning the wall of Connections — bait them past instead.

Complete Crossroads while being insane

Stay insane for the whole chapter: take the High Dose Medication, answer all four phones, and don't touch a record player after the pills — saving cures the Madness and breaks the route.

Complete Regression without killing the Tree Lurker

Don't pull any valve until the final one at the end of the level — the Radio Voice will tell you to, ignore it — and spare the Tree Lurker.

Complete Chaos without killing the Forgotten Ones

Take the alternate paths around every Forgotten One in Chaos — killing even one locks this out.

Finish Total Chaos by getting the Good Ending

The merciful run: ignore the Radio Voice, spare or bypass every boss and Forgotten One, and do the chapter mercies below. Missing even one locks the good ending out for that playthrough.

Endings 3 achievements ·

Finish Total Chaos

Finish the game — any ending counts.

Finish Total Chaos in Survivalist Mode

Finish the game on Survivalist difficulty: far fewer resources, no autosave (you spend disc records to save), and enemies hit much harder. It has a reputation for soft-locks — keep spare saves, and if the Chapter 4 record player freezes you, save with a disc record, quit, and reload. Tip: drop everything before putting on the radiation suit in Chaos, then pick it all back up — equipping it wipes your inventory.

Leave the island with your boat

Do this first: at the very start of a fresh run, walk forward to your boat and leave. It ends the game on the spot.

Combat 9 achievements ·

Repel a monster attack by parrying

Deflect a monster's attack with your pickaxe — the strengthened pickaxe from the Coastline Bench works for this. Don't kill it if you're on the merciful run.

Kill a monster

Kill any monster. Kills count against the merciful run — do your killing on the journal or Survivalist run.

Kill at least 3 monsters with only 1 Nail Bomb

Lure at least three monsters close together, then detonate a single Nail Bomb among them.

Kill a monster by tossing a weapon at it

Throw a weapon (a crafted shiv works) at a monster to finish it off.

Kill a Screamer

Kill a Screamer — the shrieking flyers in the dark areas (the flying monster in Forgotten's flooded hall is the first one you'll meet). Killing it conflicts with the merciful run, so do it on the journal run.

Impale 3 harpoons into a single monster

Stick three harpoons into one monster — load the harpoon (picked up in Forgotten) and fire all three into the same target before it dies.

Escape the first Brute in Arrival without taking damage

In Arrival, avoid the first Brute entirely — don't fight it, pull the switches and hide behind the rolling gate until you can move on. Reload your save if it lands a hit.

Die by being burned by a Slammer

Let a Slammer burn you to death — just stand in its fire until you drop.

Die by drowning

Stay underwater too long — in Forgotten's flooded sections, don't surface and you'll drown.

Crafting & survival 7 achievements ·

Exceed your inventory weight limit

Pick up items until you go over your weight limit — the game will tell you you're overencumbered.

Craft an item

Craft anything at a workbench — glass or metal shivs in Decay are the first chance.

Craft something from a syringe

Craft an item that uses a syringe as an ingredient at a workbench.

Upgrade a melee weapon with tape and barbed wire

At a workbench, upgrade a melee weapon with tape and barbed wire.

Cook a monster meat

Cook monster meat on the gas cooker (picked up in Crossroads) — heat it until it's done.

Stop your bleeding with a bandage

When you're bleeding, use a bandage from your inventory. Bandages are craftable from Decay on.

Clear your Madness level at a save zone

Let your Madness build up, then clear it at a save zone (a shrine or record player). Note: clearing Madness this way can break the Crossroads insane run — do that one without saving.

Collectibles 3 achievements ·

Find the cassette tape under the graveyard in Decay

In Decay's graveyard, break the boards on the ground to reach the secret area underneath and grab Leda's cassette tape. Also needed for the good ending.

Open Miller's storage in Decay

In Decay, drop through the vertical tunnel and pick up Miller's Key behind the rolling gate, then use it to open Miller's storage.

Complete the Journal with everything there is to find in Fort Oasis

Collect all 45 journals: Arrival 5, Decay 8, Forgotten 7, Storm 7, Crossroads 5, Regression 4, Madness 5, Chaos 3, Resolve 1. One journal in Forgotten sits behind the Protector fight — killing her for it locks out the good ending, so the full journal and the good ending need separate playthroughs; combine the journal run with Survivalist to save a run.
