Order: Sections run in walkthrough order — Golem, Forest, Desert, Tech, then the final boss and a New Game+ speedrun. Playthrough 1 is the careful run: under 40 deaths, no-hit boss kills, and the broom finish on every boss. The prologue is a survive-as-long-as-you-can tutorial — grab what you can before you die. Cumulative trackers (kills, floors, notebook, decorations) carry across the whole run.

Scope: All 57 achievements / 1,000G — the full game, no DLC. The Between Dimensions DLC has its own achievements and is out of scope. Two playthroughs: a careful first run, then a New Game+ speedrun; 25–30 hours.

Playthrough 1 · Golem Dungeon 20 achievements ·

Get money from a tree

After the prologue, hit the trees around town until one drops coins — try the main square and the lower edge of town.

Earn money from trees 100 times

Keep hitting town trees whenever you're back from a dungeon. The counter is glitchy and can pop early or late.

Make 20 visitors abandon your shop

Put 20 cheap items out at 1 gold each, one per table, and let customers wait in line until they leave without being served. Takes more than one day — sleep and repeat.

Sell 20 items slightly overpriced

Sell 20 items at the 'expensive' price, one step above optimal — the walkthrough's price tables show the band for each item.

Receive 60 really expensive valuations

Price items 'too expensive' and wait for the frowning-face valuation, 60 times. Grind it alongside Evil Merchant.

Sell 40 items really cheap

Sell 40 items at the 'cheap' price.

Sell 20 items for the perfect price

Sell 20 items at the 'perfect' (optimal) price — use the walkthrough's price tables.

Die with your inventory full of items

Fill every inventory slot, then die in the dungeon. Items in the top row survive, so put anything precious there first.

Arrive at Crazy Pete's Special Place

In any Crazy Pete resting room, walk into the top-right corner — the wet spot by the wall. It can also pop glitched alongside Rage Against the Desert.

Kill 100 bugs

Run or roll over the tiny bugs scurrying across dungeon floors — snails and beetles in the Forest, scorpions and dung beetles in the Desert.

Find a secret room 10 times

Some rooms with pitfalls sparkle for a few seconds after you enter — drop into any pitfall there. 10 secret rooms in total.

Defeat three waves of enemies in a Wave Room

In a wave room, activate the stone-slime pillar and beat 3 waves. Don't open the chest first or the pillar implodes and the waves are gone.

Use the Emblem in the room before the boss room

In the room right before a boss room, use the merchant Emblem to teleport back to town.

Hit the Witch's slime 42 times

In the Witch's shop, hit the slime in the pen in the left corner 42 times — only hits that make it react count, and the tracker is glitchy.

Collect more than 5000 gold

Cumulative gold collected from selling — comes naturally while working the Golem Dungeon.

Purchase the second shop upgrade

Buy the first shop-size upgrade on the Town Board — the shop's starting size counts as the first upgrade.

Arrive at the third floor of the Golem Dungeon 10 times

Reach floor 3 of the Golem Dungeon 10 times — comes with the material farming runs.

Kill 1000 golem enemies

1,000 Golem Dungeon kills — comes with the floor-3 repeats and material farming.

Defeat the Golem King

Beat the Golem King. Three repeating phases: ground pound and shockwave, slime-fist swipe, then the falling fist — the shockwave after the rock-fall doesn't count as a hit.

Perfect Golem King

Beat him without taking a single hit. Set a portal in the room before the boss, quit to the menu from town as a checkpoint, and retry from there — it doesn't work if you die. Maxed armour and Giant Sword; time charged attacks to the falling-fist strikes for i-frames.

Forest Dungeon 9 achievements ·

Buy more than 10 items at Le Retailer

Buy 11 items from Le Retailer (daytime, north-west of town). TA says more than 10; the walkthrough's 'buy ten' may already do it.

Purchase the third shop upgrade

Buy the second shop upgrade — it adds customer requests, waiting at the right side of the shop. Check the calendar page in your notebook and only take requests you can fill easily.

Take potions from breakables 20 times

Potions from breakable objects — urns, corpses, rocks, bushes — 20 times. Comes naturally across the dungeons.

Collect more than 50,000 gold

Cumulative 50,000 gold collected from selling.

Collect more than 100,000 gold

Cumulative 100,000 gold collected from selling.

Arrive at the third floor of the Forest Dungeon 10 times

Reach floor 3 of the Forest Dungeon 10 times.

Kill 1000 forest enemies

1,000 Forest Dungeon kills.

Defeat Carnivorous Mutae

Beat Carnivorous Mutae. Gloves up close for phase 1; bow or quick combos later. Watch for the spiral seed shots in the final phase, and ignore the respawning minions — their shots don't pass the bushes.

Perfect Carnivorous Mutae

No-hit kill — the second hardest perfect. Same portal checkpoint trick. Its minions shoot from random angles; leave them alone and focus the boss.

Desert Dungeon 7 achievements ·

Collect more than 1,000,000 gold

Hold 1,000,000 gold on you at once — this one is not cumulative. Don't spend below it until it pops.

Earn money from the Banker 5 times

Recruit the Banker (the last merchant), lend him gold, and collect a profit 5 times. Aim for +15% returns; he has a cooldown between payouts.

Purchase the fourth shop upgrade

Buy the third shop upgrade — it adds a shop assistant. It takes 30% of daily earnings and fails requests, so most players never use it.

Arrive at the third floor of the Desert Dungeon 10 times

Reach floor 3 of the Desert Dungeon 10 times.

Kill 1000 desert enemies

1,000 Desert Dungeon kills.

Defeat Naja

Beat Naja. Destroy the body parts in order 3, 2, 1, 4 — top priority is the flame-spirit part — then the head, which only charges at that point.

Perfect Naja

No-hit kill, the hardest one — Naja's attacks are pure RNG, so watch each body part's tell and use the portal checkpoint. This is the rarest achievement in the game.

Tech Dungeon & Finale 19 achievements ·

Craft all big swords

Craft all 9 big swords, Training through Fusion and Storm. Check the merchant log — the tracker is glitchy; wait for each weapon to register before upgrading further.

Craft all bows

Craft all 9 bows, Training through Exeter and Lightning. Same glitch warning as the other weapon trees.

Craft all gloves

Craft all 9 gloves, Training through Star Platinum and Thunder.

Craft all short swords

Craft all 9 short swords, Training through King and Vampire.

Craft all spears

Craft all 9 spears, Training through Fighter and Lightning Rod. The Broom Spear is your starting weapon and already in your log.

Craft all weapons

Pops when the last of the five weapon-type achievements pops — every weapon in all five trees, found or crafted.

Purchase the last shop upgrade

Buy the final shop upgrade on the Town Board.

Buy all improvements on the Town Board

Buy every Town Board improvement — all shop upgrades, all merchants, chest, bed, cash register and discount bin. The board shows what's left.

Read all Crazy Pete's notes

Read all 12 of Crazy Pete's notes — one per floor in each dungeon, in the abandoned-campsite rooms.

Complete the Notebook

Fill the Notebook — every item in all four dungeons, including each dungeon's unique ring from purple chests.

Buy all decorative items

Buy every decorative item from the Peddler — new ones unlock as you reach higher floors and beat bosses.

Kill all bosses with the broom (last hit)

Land the killing blow on all four bosses with the Broom Spear. Do the perfect kill first, then reload the portal checkpoint and redo it with the broom — the other way round can glitch the tracker.

Kill a total of 10,000 enemies

10,000 kills total. Grind spot: a Tech Dungeon room with a slime and a regenerator — park a shooting pet to kill the respawning slime. Five to eight hours by hand otherwise.

Arrive at the third floor of the Tech Dungeon 10 times

Reach floor 3 of the Tech Dungeon 10 times.

Kill 1000 tech enemies

1,000 Tech Dungeon kills.

Defeat Energy Flux

Beat Energy Flux — the easiest boss. Hide behind your shield and chip away; only the rush attack needs a dodge.

Perfect Energy Flux

No-hit kill. The shield blocks everything except the rush attack — face the boss behind the shield and strike between attacks.

Finish the game

Beat the final boss, the Space Pirate Captain — two phases, a sword alien then the big form. Tank it with hero potions and your best armour. Killing him ends the game, so finish everything else first.

Complete the game with under 40 deaths

Finish playthrough 1 with fewer than 40 deaths. Don't risk cheap deaths — teleport out of bad rooms instead of pushing on.

Second Playthrough · New Game+ 2 achievements ·

Beat the game in under 10 hours

New Game+: beat the game in under 10 hours. Gold doubles, you keep your gear, and +1 items drop — about 3 hours if you rush and skip selling. Carry red crystals and map potions from the Witch.

Complete all achievements

Pops when the last achievement does — after the New Game+ final boss.
