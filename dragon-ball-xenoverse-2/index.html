Order: Story sagas first, then class tests, Parallel Quests and Expert Missions, online, and the Conton City cleanup. Instructors' lessons mean earning their skills, not maxing friendship. The four Shenron wishes for new characters double as progress toward the character collection.

Scope: Base game only: 50 achievements / 1,000G. The 11 DLC achievements (300G across 4 packs, including level 99 and the Hero Colosseum) are out of scope. No TrueAchievements walkthrough exists (it needs a new owner); route notes are paraphrased from the GameFAQs trophy guide. About 60–80 hours.
