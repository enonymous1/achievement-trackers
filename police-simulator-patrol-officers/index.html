Police Simulator: Patrol Officers — Achievement Tracker

Patrol Brighton's streets, earn Duty Stars, and keep the Melting Pot clean — one justified action at a time.

Order: Sections follow the Steam 100% guide's grouping: the five first shifts in area order, then the Duty Star unlocks, then the four systems — arrests, first aid, towing, photography, and the radar. The cumulative totals build up across a single career; the two 'in a single shift' achievements need a long shift. Only justified actions count — a random stop and search costs Citizen Points even if you find something.

Scope: All 25 achievements / 1,000G — the full game, no DLC. No TrueAchievements walkthrough exists; route notes are paraphrased from the Steam 100% Achievement Guide (Blahaj.exe, 2026). About 12–15 hours.

First shifts 5 achievements ·

Start your first shift in North Point.

North Point is the first area, open from the start — just start a shift there.

Start your first shift in Beaufort Landing.

Earn Duty Stars in the district to unlock Beaufort Landing, then start a shift there.

Start your first shift in Chester.

Earn Duty Stars in the district to unlock Chester, then start a shift there.

Start your first shift in Historic Downtown.

Earn Duty Stars in the district to unlock Historic Downtown, then start a shift there.

Start your first shift in Alcombey.

The last area to unlock — keep earning Duty Stars until Alcombey opens, then start a shift there.

Unlocks 2 achievements ·

Collect your first Duty Star.

Earn 150 XP from callouts and interactions during shifts — that is your first Duty Star.

Unlock your first patrol car.

Reach the 3rd Duty Star (650 XP) to unlock the Cruiser.

Arrests 6 achievements ·

Justifiably arrest a person for driving under the influence.

Pull a vehicle over with cause, then run a drug or alcohol test — arrest them if it comes back positive.

Justifiably arrest 3 people in a single shift.

Work a long shift and pull over everyone you can justify — three justified arrests before the shift ends.

Justifiably arrest a person with an open warrant on them.

Check a car's license plate and look it up before you approach — a warrant shows there. Or check IDs at pull-overs and stops.

Justifiably arrest 30 people over multiple shifts in a single career.

Arrest only when you have cause — DUI, illegal items, stolen or fake IDs, stolen vehicles, open warrants. A random stop and search costs Citizen Points even if you find something.

Justifiably arrest 40 people for driving under the influence over multiple shifts in a single career

Drug, amphetamine, and alcohol DUIs all count — test everyone you justifiably pull over. The legal alcohol limit is 0.08%; anything above it is arrestable.

Justifiably arrest 50 people over multiple shifts in a single career.

The career arrest total — keep working justified arrests and it comes with the rest.

First aid 3 achievements ·

Justifiably call for an ambulance.

At a major accident, go to a person on the ground, interact, and choose 'Release to Ambulance' then 'Dismiss and call Medic'.

Justifiable call for an ambulance 20 times over multiple shifts in a single career.

Twenty justified ambulance calls across your career.

Justifiably call for an ambulance 30 times over multiple shifts in a single career.

Run long shifts so more accidents come in. Once the injured person is in the ambulance you can leave the scene without finishing it.

Towing 3 achievements ·

Justifiably order a car towed.

Order a tow with cause — a parking violation (blocking a fire hydrant or handicap spot), an undrivable car after an accident, or a driver who was arrested.

Justifiably order 30 cars towed over multiple shifts in a single career.

Thirty justified tows across your career. Towing for invalid insurance or registration needs a traffic stop or an active violation — not just a parked car with expired tags.

Justifiably order 50 cars towed over multiple shifts in a single career.

Fifty justified tows across your career.

Photography 3 achievements ·

Photograph your first piece of evidence.

At a crime scene, take an evidence marker from your car's trunk, place it by a piece of evidence, then photograph it.

Photograph 50 pieces of evidence over multiple shifts in a single career.

Evidence is anything from a crime left in the world — guns, knives, bumpers, plates, mirrors. Mark each piece first, then snap the photo.

Photograph 120 pieces of evidence over multiple shifts in a single career.

The career photo total — mark and photograph evidence at every scene you work.

Speed radar 3 achievements ·

Justifiably report 10 speeders in a single shift.

Report ten speeders before the shift ends — use the handheld radar gun or pace them in your car. A long shift gives you more chances.

Justifiable report 40 speeders over multiple shifts in a single career.

Forty reported speeders across your career.

Justifiably report 100 speeders over multiple shifts in a single career.

Aim the radar gun at a vehicle and fire, or pace it and watch your dash speed against the limit — then use the Blip button (Y / Triangle) to record the offense.
