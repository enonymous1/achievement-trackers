Pigeon Simulator — Achievement Tracker

Collect anomalies, fail once on purpose, then grind every upgrade in a single life.

0 / 10 achievements · 0 / 1000G

Order: In walkthrough order: the three tutorial gadgets first (they can be cheesed on the spot), then the first missions — including one deliberate failure — then the upgrade grind. Pops may vary slightly with the order you take days.

Scope: Full game: all 10 achievements / 1,000G. No missables; about 1–2 hours in a single playthrough.

Tutorial Cheese the three gadgets before leaving the tutorial ·

Use the Anomaly Revealer device 30 times

In the tutorial, buy the Anomaly Revealer when prompted, then use it 30 times in a row.

Use the Chaos Stabilizer 20 times

In the next tutorial room, buy the Chaos Stabilizer and press use 20 times — each press counts even where it says you can't use it here.

Use the Dimensional Seal device 10 times

Buy the Dimensional Seal and use it 10 times. If presses don't count where you bought it, carry it to the rift, close the rift with it, then use it 9 more times.

First missions One upgrade, one start, one deliberate failure, then a clean day 1 ·

Upgrade pigeon for the first time

From the main menu choose Play to reach the mission start area, then buy any upgrade at the computer in front of you.

Start the mission

Climb into the van on the right of the mission start area and start a mission.

Fail to collect the quota and fail the mission

On day 1, do nothing: leave the van, wait for chaos to build, and let a moving, attacking anomaly kill you — don't deliver anything to the collection area first. Dying resets your upgrades and drops you back to day 1, which is exactly what the route wants.

Buy any piece of equipment in the shop for the first time

After the reset, buy the chaos echo sensor from the item stand on the left of the mission start area. Equip the echo locator in the van before your next run — it helps find anomalies.

Fulfill the minimum quota on the mission

Complete day 1 properly: collect anomalies (aim for around $1,000 worth, over the quota), then go into the van and hit the leave button — leaving takes about 30 seconds.

The grind 50 anomalies and every upgrade in one life ·

Send 50 Anomalies for analysis

Keep sending anomalies for analysis across runs until you've sent 50 in total.

Fully upgrade the pigeon

Buy every upgrade in a single life, without dying — $27,860 in total. If you die on a day, dashboard and quit the game at once to roll back to before that day. Prioritize movement speed, grab the bread helmet/hat (it heals you while stood still — sit in the van to recover), switch to the Outskirts map on the second computer, and aim for $2,000–3,000 a day; expect around 10–15 runs. Some upgrades sit off-screen — scroll right to see them all.
