Canterbury Trails All 15 achievements in story order ·

Complete the Prologue

Finish the prologue at the stable.

Complete the photography tutorial

Start the Scenic Photography 101 mission with Barbie's father and finish the photography tutorial.

Congratulate Lucky on a job well done

After Scenic Photography 101, use the special pet on Lucky ("super pet") at least once after finishing a quest.

Enter a changing station and change your outfit

Step into a changing station at the Ranger Station or any stable and change any piece of clothing. Outfits come from tasks, boxes lying around, and horse level-ups.

Win first place in a race

Win the story horse race in first place. Other races are marked with a checkered flag on the map, and only one race is mandatory.

Find the Silver Canyon Mustang

Finish Dr. Potts' Mystery Horse questline, through the second artifact, the backpack quest, and the website pictures to the final artifact and the thrilling conclusion.

Complete a mission for Ranger Quinn

Finish your first quest for Ranger Quinn (Trail Trek).

Complete a mission for Ranger Rory

Finish your first quest for Ranger Rory.

Complete a mission for Dr. Green

Finish your first quest for Dr. Green.

Reach max level for Horse Bond

Raise Lucky's bond to max level 11: pet her when she asks, brush her, feed her everything you receive, and keep her clean.

Complete a mission for Stella

For Stella, recreate the Big Dipper constellation at her site.

Complete all the missions for Barbie's friends

Finish every side quest. They are the "!" markers on the map; press Options to track one (blue marker).

Complete the park packet

Fill the park packet with main and side quests and photos of animals, people, and viewpoints. If the story stalls on the packet bar, clear side quests first, then return to the main quests.

Get together for the big group photo

Story mission near the end: just follow the quest marker to the big group photo.

Complete the game

Finish the game: go to Barbie on the last quest and let the conversation play out.
