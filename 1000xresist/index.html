Find "Green Heaven" in the Orchard

From the waterfall on Level 1, turn right at sign '1' (don't take the stairs), pass sign 'C', the ALLMOTHER heads, and the alcove; at the spine statue turn left into the 'Grove' doorway, then left up the escalators.

Find the horse statue in the Orchard

Take stairs 'D' by the waterfall, turn right, and follow the overhead signs to the Horse Lobby.

Find the pillar room in the Orchard

Left of the waterfall into the glass walkway (Shrine Courtyard), straight past the white arch, right up the wooden spiral stairs, then the grey stairs on the left — don't cross the bridge — into the prayer room.

Find the skull shrine in the Orchard

Left of the waterfall into the Shrine Courtyard, first right through the white arch.

Find the mirror shrine in the Orchard

Take stairs 'D' by the waterfall and turn left toward the mirror shrine.

Endings 8 achievements ·

An Ending: Scorched Earth

In the epilogue, turn off every light (Parents may stay on) and confirm with Secretary.

An Ending: Her Name Is Not Mauve

In the epilogue, leave Mauve's light on and confirm with Secretary.

An Ending: The Law

In the epilogue, turn off Mauve's light (Red Guard stays on) and confirm with Secretary.

An Ending: Lights Out

In the epilogue, turn off Mauve, Red Guard, and Sisters, then confirm with Secretary.

Ending: Epitaphs (Blue)

In the epilogue, turn off Mauve, Red Guard, and Principal (Sisters and Jiaos on), then confirm with Secretary.

Ending: Epitaphs (Red)

In the epilogue, turn off Mauve and Red Guard only (Sisters, Jiaos, and Principal on), then confirm with Secretary.

Complete The Game

View the epilogue through either Epitaphs ending. Watch all five An Endings first — they return you to the final scene so you can change the lights; after your first finish, Chapter 10 via chapter select lets you collect the rest.
