Winter Burrow — Achievement Tracker

A little mouse rebuilds a burrow through a long winter — follow the owl, find Aunty, and furnish the house.

Gamerscore progress

0 / 32 achievements · 0 / 1000G

Order: In walkthrough order, following the story pages: the beginning, following the owl, Aunty's recovery, rebuilding the burrow, then the furnishing cleanup. Cumulative achievements (wood, kills, cooking, furniture) pop whenever their totals land, so they may arrive earlier or later than listed.

Scope: Full game: all 32 achievements / 1,000G. No missables; about 8–10 hours in a single playthrough.

The Beginning 5 achievements ·

Place a bed in the burrow

Craft a Simple Bed at the woodworking table (4 twigs, 3 flax fiber, 2 beech wood) and place it in your home.

Repair the armchair

Repair the Dusty Armchair (2 beech planks, 4 twigs, 10 flax fiber) — this also unlocks the knitting recipes.

Repair the stove in the burrow

Repair the stove with 8 pebbles gathered outside the house.

Repair the bridge to Aunty

Repair the broken bridge south-east of your house (5 beech planks, 5 flax rope), then cross to Aunty.

Unlock the door to the basement

Use the Basement Key from Aunty on the basement door to the left of the house.

Follow the Owl 4 achievements ·

Discover the Gnarled Oaks

Cross the bridge past Aunty's collapsed home into the new area to discover the Gnarled Oaks.

Discover the Shadow Pines

Head north past Bufo into the next area to discover the Shadow Pines.

Build a campfire in the wilderness

Use a campfire kit from a backpack north-east of Gnawtusk's area to build a campfire in the wilderness.

Bring Aunty back home

Finish Gnawtusk's herb stashes (by the pine tree, by the shell, by the funny-looking rock), then talk to Aunty back home.

Aunty's Recovery 8 achievements ·

Collect 100 pieces of wood

Collect 100 pieces of wood — it comes naturally while gathering for the planter boxes and repairs.

Unlock first floor in the burrow

Repair the stairs to the first floor (15 oak planks, 5 pine planks).

Knit 8 clothing pieces

Knit 8 clothing pieces — start with the linen set, then wool clothes from fur tufts once you have enough yarn.

Complete Bufo's quests

Finish Bufo's quests: fetch pebbles and twigs, find Pollywog south of the water, bake Blackberry and Rowanberry pies, and deliver Bufo's key to Pollywog.

Discover the White Pillars

Head south-west past the spider area to discover the White Pillars, collecting birch wood there.

Craft all pickaxes

Craft all pickaxes, ending with the Flint Pickaxe once Willow's area gives you birch wood.

Craft all axes

Craft all axes, ending with the Flint Axe.

Complete Willow's quests

Finish Willow's quests: return their notebook (found outside your house), find the tunnel entrance north-east of home with the Flint Shovel, recover the helmet artifact, and hand over hazelnuts.

Rebuilding the Burrow 6 achievements ·

Kill 100 creatures

Kill 100 creatures — beetles, ants, and spiders as you travel; it usually pops while gathering for Aunty's burrow.

Repair Aunty's broken house

Rebuild Aunty's burrow: 10 birch planks, 20 oak planks, 20 granite, 10 silk yarn (spiders drop silk — cut their webs if you'd rather not fight them).

Complete Gnawtusk's quests

Finish Gnawtusk's quests by handing over the rare herbs you picked up in backpacks, then invite him to the feast.

Spin 50 balls of yarn

Spin 50 balls of yarn at the armchair — it builds up naturally while knitting clothes and the Warm Shoes.

Complete Moss quests

Finish Moss's quests: bring the white fox fur tufts, find his missing key, show him Aunty's Old Shawl, then bring back Pinesap's pendant from the dark area west of the hood.

Have a big dinner at Aunty's

Invite everyone — Gnawtusk, Bufo, Willow, and Moss — then return to Aunty to trigger the feast and the story ending.

Furnishings for a Mouse 9 achievements ·

Cook 100 recipes

Cook 100 recipes — Roasted Chanterelle and Blue Tea are the cheapest to spam, and 'Craft All' speeds it up.

Craft 8 pieces of the twig furniture set

Craft 8 twig furniture pieces — Twig Side Tables use the least materials.

Craft 9 pieces of the fibre furniture set

Craft 9 fibre pieces — Round Fibre Rugs are the cheapest, though they eat a lot of flax rope.

Craft 150 woodworking recipes

Craft 150 woodworking recipes — it usually pops while working through the furniture sets.

Craft 9 pieces of the pine furniture set

Craft 9 pine pieces — Pine Chairs are the simplest.

Craft 9 pieces of the birch furniture set

Craft 9 birch pieces — Birch Stools are the easiest.

Craft 30 furniture pieces

Craft 30 furniture pieces in total — it pops while working through the sets.

Craft 9 pieces of the pebble furniture set

Craft 9 pebble pieces — Pebble Stools are the easiest.

Craft 9 pieces of the oak furniture set

Craft 9 oak pieces — Oak Tables are the easiest.
